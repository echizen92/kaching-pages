Common Questions

How do I set up automatic transaction logging?
Open the app, go to the Shortcuts Setup screen (available from the Transactions tab), and follow the step-by-step guide to create an iOS Shortcut that logs Apple Pay transactions automatically.

How do I unlock Pro?
Kaching Pro is available via in-app purchase. You can view features and subscribe from the paywall screen in the app.

Where is my data stored?
All your data is stored locally on your device. Kaching does not require a bank account and does not collect personal data to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I reset or delete my data?
You can delete the app to erase all local data, or use the debug menu (ladybug icon on the Home screen) to clear data during testing.
