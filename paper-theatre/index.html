Pop-up fairy tales that unfold in your hands. Open your iPhone and the story stands up: castles, beanstalks and lanterns rise out of the fold, and the pieces swing, glow and move as you read.

Jack and the Beanstalk is free. The Night Post is an original story, available to buy, and more classic fairy tales are on the way.

Help

How do I turn the page?

Swipe the lower page, or tap the curled corner. On a foldable iPhone you can also fold the book partway and flick.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to the library, open Settings (a grown-up answers the parental gate) and tap Restore purchases.

Can my family share the stories I buy?

Yes. Stories you buy are shared with your Family Sharing group automatically.

Privacy

Paper Theatre collects nothing about you or your child. See the Privacy Policy.
