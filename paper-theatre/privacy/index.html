Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Paper Theatre is a pop-up storybook app for children and the grown-ups who read with them. It is published by Faiz Rashid in Singapore, the maker of Kachingz.

What we collect

Nothing. Paper Theatre does not collect or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or your child to us or to anyone else.

The app saves a few settings on the device only: the narrator voice, your place in each book, and the screen layout. Nothing leaves the device, and deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Stories are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks with Apple which stories your family owns so it can unlock them. Purchases are shared through Family Sharing if you use it.

A parental gate appears before any purchase, and before any link that leads out of the app.

Children

Paper Theatre is designed for children and follows the App Store's Kids Category rules. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
