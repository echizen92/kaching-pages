Support

Folio is a day planner for iPhone, bound in leather. Your calendar, tasks, notes and habits sit together on paper pages, and everything you write stays on your iPhone.

Folio is free to use. Folio Complete, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, adds more leathers, Ask Folio, handwriting, the Private pocket, desk and tent modes on iPhone Duo, the focus timer, widgets and export.

Email faiz.rashid@gmx.us and we'll get back to you. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you tapped. A screenshot helps.

Help

Do I need an account?

No. Folio has no accounts and nothing to sign in to. Your planner is kept on your iPhone.

How do I back up my planner?

On the Today page, tap Cover and settings at the foot of the page, then use the backup section. Folio also keeps its own recent backups on your iPhone. To keep a copy somewhere else, share a backup to Files or another place you trust, and restore it from there.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

On the new iPhone, choose Moving from another iPhone? on the cover when Folio first opens. On the old iPhone, open Cover and settings and choose Move to a New iPhone, then scan the code it shows with the new iPhone. The planner goes straight across between the two phones, over an encrypted connection. Keep both phones close together.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Cover and settings and tap Folio Complete at the top, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

In the iPhone's Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Folio Complete. You can also go to your App Store subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Everything you made stays in your planner.

Why does Ask Folio say "No answer"?

Ask Folio uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Without it, Folio still searches your planner for the words you typed.

How do I use desk and tent modes?

They are for iPhone Duo. Half-fold it with the hinge across and rest it on a table for desk mode: the lower half shows your focus timer and today's tasks. Stand it up like a tent so the cover display faces you for a tear-off daily calendar. Every other part of Folio works the same on any iPhone.

I can't open my locked notes or Private pocket.

They open with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone's passcode. If Folio asks for your recovery words (for example after the iPhone's passcode was turned off, or after restoring onto another iPhone), enter the words you wrote down when you set them up. Without the recovery words, locked notes and pocket cards cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

The weather doesn't show.

Tap Show the weather on the Today page and allow location while using Folio. You can change this later in the iPhone's Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Location Services.

Privacy

Folio has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we never receive your planner. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
