Folio Complete

Folio is free to use. Folio Complete is optional and is offered two ways:

  • Yearly: an auto-renewing subscription for one year at a time, with a free first week for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the free week ends, or when you confirm the purchase if there is no free week. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current year, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.
  • Lifetime: a single purchase, with no renewals.

Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, in your local currency. Refunds are handled by Apple. If Folio Complete ends, everything you made stays in your planner.

Suggestions and answers

Scrap suggestions and Ask Folio answers are written automatically from your own entries and can be wrong. Check anything important, such as dates, times and amounts, before you rely on it.

Your planner

Your planner is kept on your iPhone, and we cannot recover it for you. Keep a backup somewhere you trust, and write down your recovery words if you lock notes or use the Private pocket.
