Kaching — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaching ("we", "the app") handles your information. Kaching is designed to be privacy-first: it runs mostly on your device and does not require linking a bank account.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data to our own servers. The app has no account system and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

The only information that leaves your device is:

  • AI Insights requests — when you tap "Generate" on Daily Tips, What-If Scenarios, Predictions, or the Weekly Insight, the app sends a summary of your spending (aggregated category totals, no names or identifiers) to Kaching's AI proxy server, which forwards it to the DeepSeek AI service to produce personalized tips. The proxy stores only an anonymous usage counter (no request content) and enforces a monthly cap. These requests are used solely to generate the insight text shown to you and are not sold or shared.
  • Pro AI analytics — when you use the paid advanced analytics reports (Analytics AI / detailed insights), the app also sends the merchant names referenced by those reports to the AI service (ai.kachingz.com → DeepSeek) so it can produce the narrative for you (for example, "your S$350 pending is mostly this week's Grab rides"). That data is processed transiently to generate your report and is not stored, sold, or used for marketing. The proxy does not retain request content; its log records only metadata (timestamp, anonymous ID, status, character count, tokens used, latency).

2. Data Stored On Your Device

All app data (transactions, budgets, income, your pet, avatars, photos you choose for your avatar) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

  • Photos: If you pick a photo from your library to use as your avatar, the image is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • iCloud backup: Data may be included in your device's standard iCloud backup as controlled by your own iOS settings.

3. Subscriptions

Kaching Pro is a subscription processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. Kaching never sees your payment card details. Subscription management is handled through your App Store account settings.

4. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for a general audience (rated 4+). It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Rights

Because we store data on your device and collect nothing to our servers, you control your data at all times. You may delete the app to erase all local data.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be linked from the App Store listing.
